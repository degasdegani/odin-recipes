Lasanha da Nona

Description

Nona's lasagna is a classic baked pasta dish made of layers of tender pasta, rich sauce, and melted cheese. Each slice comes out golden on top and soft in the middle, with the comforting flavor of a proper family meal.

It takes a little patience to assemble, but the result is worth it: a hearty dish that is made to be shared at the table.

Ingredients

  • 500 g lasagna pasta sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 700 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 400 g mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • 100 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Baby spinach leaves and red onion slices, for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Cook the pasta sheets in salted boiling water according to the package instructions, then drain.
  6. Spread a thin layer of sauce in a baking dish, then add a layer of pasta, more sauce, and some mozzarella. Repeat until you run out of ingredients.
  7. Finish the top with sauce, the remaining mozzarella, and the grated Parmesan.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for another 15 minutes, until golden.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
  10. Serve each slice with spinach leaves and red onion slices on the side.
